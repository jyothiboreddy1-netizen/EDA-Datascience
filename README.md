# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis using Python

## 📌 Project Overview
This project performs **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on a telecom customer churn dataset to uncover the key factors influencing customer attrition. The analysis helps identify customer behavior patterns and provides actionable business insights that can help organizations improve customer retention.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Understand customer demographics and service usage.
- Identify the major reasons behind customer churn.
- Analyze relationships between different customer attributes.
- Generate business insights to support retention strategies.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Google Colab
- Pandas

- Seaborn

---

## 📂 Dataset
**Dataset:** Customer Churn Dataset

The dataset contains customer demographic information, subscription details, billing information, and churn status.

---

## 📊 Analysis Performed
- Data Loading
- Data Cleaning
- Missing Value Analysis
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Univariate Analysis
- Bivariate Analysis
- Correlation Analysis
- Visualization of Customer Behavior
- Business Insight Generation

---

## 📈 Key Business Insights

- 👴 Senior citizens are more likely to churn compared to younger customers.
- 💍 Customers without partners show higher churn rates.
- 📅 Customers with month-to-month contracts have the highest probability of churn.
- 💳 Customers paying through Electronic Check tend to churn more frequently.
- 💰 Higher monthly charges are associated with higher churn.
- ⏳ Customers with shorter tenure are significantly more likely to leave.
- 🌐 Fiber optic internet customers show relatively higher churn compared to DSL users.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Offer long-term contract discounts.
- Provide loyalty rewards for customers with low tenure.
- Improve customer support for high-risk customer groups.
- Review Electronic Check payment experience.
- Introduce personalized retention offers for customers with high monthly charges.
